# ITFE Exam Preparation Guide

I'm taking the **ITFE(Fundamental Information Technology Engineer)** exam this coming **October 25**, and like many people, I realized I don't have enough time to read every page of the textbooks and make my own notes.

With only about **2 months left**, I wanted a study guide that focuses on what matters most.

So I started creating my own cheat sheets and study materials with the help of **Claude AI**, while reviewing and organizing everything according to my own study plan.

Instead of keeping them to myself, I decided to share them with anyone who's in the same situation.

If you're also preparing for ITFE with limited time, I hope this repository saves you some time and helps you study more efficiently.

---

## What's in this repository?

This repository will gradually grow as I continue preparing for the exam.

Currently it contains:

- A complete study plan
- Easy-to-read explanations
- Important concepts and summaries
- Practice questions and quizzes
- Exam tips and reminders

More content will be added every week.

## Why I made this

Most ITFE resources are either:

- too detailed,
- scattered across different websites,
- or difficult to finish when you're short on time.

I wanted everything in one place with a simple goal:

> Learn what's important, practice consistently, and walk into the exam with confidence.

This repository isn't meant to replace the official books. It's simply a study companion that I personally use every day.

---

## Who is this for?

Anyone preparing for the ITFE exam, especially if you:

- only have a few months left,
- don't know where to start,
- prefer organized notes instead of long textbooks,
- or just want someone else's study companion.
